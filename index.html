          How to play

          Pick up to five cards and play them as a poker hand. Its chips × mult is added to your score.

          Reach the target within four hands. Discards swap cards without scoring.

          pikachu.js
          Level 1 · Still

          Join pairs of matching monsters to clear the board.

          Score 0
          Best -
          Pairs 0
          Level 1 / 13 Still
          Time 4:00
          How to play

          Tap two matching monsters to clear them. The line between them bends at most twice and only crosses empty space, the gap around the board included.

          Clear every pair before the time runs out. Quick matches in a row score a combo.

          Each level sets a gravity: after a match the other tiles stay put, fall, rise, slide, or split two ways.

          Arrows and Z work too; H hints, P pauses.
